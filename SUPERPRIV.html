Protection Basics

A pledge to commit to the habits below — it's a commitment you make, not an automatic shield.

Safe Mode Pledge NOT PLEDGED
Turn this on once you've committed to following the habits in the cards below.

🔑 Use Strong, Unique Passwords

  • At least 12 characters with letters, numbers, and symbols
  • Never reuse passwords across accounts
  • Use a password manager to keep track

📱 Secure Your Devices

  • Enable screen lock (PIN, pattern, or biometrics)
  • Install apps only from official stores
  • Keep your operating system updated

✉️ Enable Two-Factor Authentication

  • Adds a second step beyond your password
  • Use an authenticator app over SMS when possible
  • Save backup codes somewhere safe

📶 Be Smart on Public Wi-Fi

  • Avoid logging into sensitive accounts on public networks
  • Use a VPN if you must connect
  • Turn off auto-connect to open networks
